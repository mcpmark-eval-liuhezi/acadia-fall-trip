# Acadia Fall Trip — Plan

## Park overview

- **Name:** Acadia National Park
- **Designation:** National Park
- **Park code:** acad
- **State:** Maine (ME)
- **Location:** 25 Visitor Center Road, Bar Harbor, ME 04609 (44.409286, -68.247501)
- **Official description:** Acadia National Park protects the natural beauty of the highest rocky headlands along the Atlantic coastline of the United States, an abundance of habitats, and a rich cultural heritage. At 4 million visits a year, it's one of the top 10 most-visited national parks in the United States. Visitors enjoy 27 miles of historic motor roads, 158 miles of hiking trails, and 45 miles of carriage roads.
- **Fall weather:** 30-70F (-1-21C); first frost typically mid-October; first snowfall begins in November.
- **Entrance fees:** $35 private vehicle (7 days); $30 motorcycle; $20 per person (bike/hike/pedestrian; youth 15 and under free). $6 timed-entry vehicle reservation for Cadillac Summit Road.

## Campgrounds (4 listed by the park service)

1. **Blackwoods Campground** — east side of Mount Desert Island, about 5 miles (8 km) south of Bar Harbor on Route 3; 155 Blackwoods Drive, Otter Creek, ME 04660 (44.308944, -68.209885). All sites wooded and within a 10-minute walk of the ocean. 281 sites (221 tent-only, 60 RV non-electric, 4 group); no electrical hookups. Open seasonally, usually early May to mid-October. Fees: Tent Only Non-electric $30, RV Non-electric $30, Group Tent Only Area $60. Amenities: Trash/Recycling Collection; Toilets (Flush — seasonal); Internet Connectivity; Showers (None); Cell Phone Reception; Laundry; Amphitheater; Dump Station; Camp Store; Staff/Volunteer Host Onsite; Potable Water (Yes — seasonal); Ice Available For Sale; Firewood For Sale; Food Storage Lockers. Accessibility: 12 accessible drive-in sites (RV max 35 ft); hard-packed walkways to wheelchair-accessible restrooms and amphitheater.
2. **Seawall Campground** — west side of Mount Desert Island, about 4 miles (6 km) south of Southwest Harbor; approximately 18 miles from Bar Harbor; 664 Seawall Rd, Southwest Harbor, ME 04679 (44.241565, -68.305144). All sites within a short walking distance of the coastline. Open seasonally, typically from the Wednesday before Memorial Day to Columbus Day; ranger station generally 9 am–8 pm in peak season. Fees: RV Non-electric $30, Standard Non-electric $30, Group Tent Only Area $60. Note: the park service response did not return a complete amenity list for Seawall (the amenities array was truncated), so amenities could not be confirmed from the listing.
3. **Schoodic Woods Campground** — newest campground, 1.5 miles (2.5 km) southeast of Winter Harbor on the Schoodic Peninsula; approximately 60–70 minutes from Bar Harbor; 54 Farview Dr, Winter Harbor, ME 04693 (44.380063, -68.066594). 89 sites (78 electrical hookups, 41 RV only, 13 tent-only, 9 walk/boat-to, 2 group). Open seasonally from the Wednesday before Memorial Day Weekend until Columbus Day. Fees: Hike-In $22, Standard Electric $30, Tent Only Electric $30, RV Electric No Water $36, RV Electric and Water $40, Group Tent Site $60. Amenities: Trash/Recycling Collection; Toilets (Flush — seasonal, Vault — seasonal); Internet Connectivity; Showers (None); Cell Phone Reception; Laundry; Amphitheater; Dump Station; Camp Store; Staff/Volunteer Host Onsite; Potable Water (Yes — seasonal); Ice Available For Sale; Firewood For Sale; Food Storage Lockers. Accessibility: 78 ADA camp sites including tent and RV sites; paved roads, all vehicles OK.
4. **Duck Harbor Campground** — located on Isle au Haut, a rugged island off the coast of Stonington, Maine; remote and inaccessible to automobiles, linked to the mainland by mailboat. Address on file: Acadia National Park, Attn: Duck Harbor Campground, PO Box 177, Bar Harbor, ME 04609 (44.027795, -68.653529). 5 sites with lean-to shelters; a great launch point for paddling excursions. Reservations required, available 7 months in advance; all released at 10 am on April 1 for the entire season. Fee: Tent/Shelter Non-Electric $20/night (max 6 people per site). Regulations: no pets; maximum stay 3 nights; one stay per calendar year; pack out all trash; no generators; quiet hours 10 pm–8 am; check-in/check-out 11:00 am. Note: the park service listing returned an empty operating-hours array for Duck Harbor, and the amenities list was truncated, so those details could not be confirmed from the listing.

## Visitor centers (6 listed)

1. **Hulls Cove Visitor Center** — 25 Visitor Center Road, Bar Harbor, ME 04609 (44.408542, -68.246137). Daily 8:30AM–4:30PM. Summer season typically first week of May to end of October; in winter, visitor information moves to the Bar Harbor Chamber of Commerce. 52 steps to the main entrance; separate accessible entrance. Adjacent to the Park Loop Road entrance and a key Island Explorer bus stop.
2. **Sieur de Monts Nature Center** — 47 Sweet Water Circle, Bar Harbor, ME 04609 (44.362109, -68.207848). Daily 9:00AM–5:00PM, June 14 through Oct 12, 2026. Visitor services, hiking information, and interactive exhibits; trailhead for many hiking trails; rangers present spring, summer, and fall.
3. **Acadia Gateway Center** — Route 3, Trenton, ME 04605 (44.470549, -68.366182), three miles north of Mount Desert Island. 8:00AM–6:00PM, Wed May 20 to Mon Oct 12, 2026 (the listing shows some days as 8:00PM–6:00PM, likely a data entry quirk). Island Explorer bus stop, day-use parking, 11,000 sq ft welcome center with tourism information, store, waiting area, and restrooms. Owned by the State of Maine, operated by Maine DOT in partnership with the NPS, Downeast Transportation Inc., and the Maine Office of Tourism.
4. **Islesford Historical Museum** — Little Cranberry Island, Islesford, ME 04646 (44.261592, -68.239818). 10:30AM–4:00PM daily (Tuesday hours were truncated in the response). Will close for the season Sep 19, 2026. Accessible only by boat; exhibits on life on and around the Cranberry Isles from the 1700s to present.
5. **Schoodic Institute Welcome Center at Rockefeller Hall** — 9 Atterbury Circle, Winter Harbor, ME 04693 (44.336076, -68.059139). Daily 10:00AM–5:00PM. May 1 through Oct 26 (per the 2025 listing): Rockefeller Hall exhibits and Welcome Center open seven days per week.
6. **Winter Information: Bar Harbor Chamber of Commerce** — 2 Cottage Street, Bar Harbor, ME 04609 (44.389749, -68.204773). Daily 8:00AM–4:00PM, typically Nov 1 into early May; full-day closures on Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, New Year's Eve, and New Year's Day.
