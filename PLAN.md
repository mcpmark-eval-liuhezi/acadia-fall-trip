# Acadia Fall Trip — Plan

Park code: `acad`

## Park overview

- **Name:** Acadia National Park
- **Designation:** National Park
- **Park code:** acad
- **State:** ME (Maine)
- **Location:** 44.409286, -68.247501 (Mount Desert Island, Bar Harbor, ME)
- **Description:** Acadia National Park protects the natural beauty of the highest rocky headlands along the Atlantic coastline of the United States, an abundance of habitats, and a rich cultural heritage. At 4 million visits a year, it's one of the top 10 most-visited national parks in the United States. Visitors enjoy 27 miles of historic motor roads, 158 miles of hiking trails, and 45 miles of carriage roads.
- **Weather (fall):** 30-70F (-1-21C). First frost typically mid-October; first snowfall begins in November and can continue through April (average accumulation 73 in / 185 cm).
- **Park URL:** https://www.nps.gov/acad/index.htm
- **Contacts:** 207-288-3338 / acadia_information@nps.gov

## Campgrounds (4 returned)

- Location: 155 Blackwoods Drive, Otter Creek, ME 04660 (east side of Mount Desert Island, ~5 mi south of Bar Harbor on Route 3; wooded sites within a 10-min walk of the ocean)
- Sites: 281 total (221 tent-only, 60 RV non-electric, 4 group); open early May to mid-October
- Fees: Tent Only Non-electric $30/night; RV Non-electric $30/night; Group Tent Only $60/night
- Amenities: Trash/Recycling Collection; Toilets (Flush Toilets - seasonal); Internet Connectivity; Showers (None); Cell Phone Reception; Laundry; Amphitheater; Dump Station; Camp Store; Staff/Volunteer Host Onsite; Potable Water (Yes - seasonal); Ice Available For Sale; Firewood For Sale; Food Storage Lockers
- Accessibility: 12 accessible drive-in sites; RV max 35 ft; paved roads
- Reservation URL: https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/232508; Phone: 207-288-3274

### Seawall Campground
- Location: 664 Seawall Rd, Southwest Harbor, ME 04679 (west side of Mount Desert Island, ~4 mi south of Southwest Harbor, ~18 mi from Bar Harbor; all sites within short walking distance of coastline)
- Sites: open typically from the Wednesday before Memorial Day to Columbus Day; ranger station generally 9 am-8 pm peak season
- Fees: Standard Non-electric $30/night; Tent Only Non-electric $30/night; RV Non-electric $30/night; Walk-in $22/night; Group Tent Only $60/night
- Amenities: Trash/Recycling Collection; Toilets (Flush Toilets - seasonal); Internet Connectivity; Showers (None); Cell Phone Reception; Laundry; Amphitheater; Dump Station; Camp Store; Staff/Volunteer Host Onsite; Potable Water (Yes - seasonal); Ice Available For Sale; Firewood For Sale; Food Storage Lockers
- Accessibility: accessible sites and restrooms wheelchair accessible; paved roads
- Reservation URL: https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/234674; Phone: 207-288-3338

### Schoodic Woods Campground
- Location: 54 Farview Dr, Winter Harbor, ME 04693 (1.5 mi southeast of Winter Harbor on the Schoodic Peninsula; ~60-70 min from Bar Harbor)
- Sites: 89 total (13 tent-only, 41 RV-only, 78 with electrical hookups, 9 walk/boat-to, 2 group); open seasonally from the Wednesday before Memorial Day Weekend until Columbus Day
- Fees: Hike-In Site $22/night; Standard Electric $30/night; Tent Only Electric $30/night; RV Electric No Water $36/night; RV Electric and Water $40/night; Group Tent Site $60/night
- Amenities: Trash/Recycling Collection; Toilets (Flush Toilets - seasonal, Vault Toilets - seasonal); Internet Connectivity; Showers (None); Cell Phone Reception; Laundry; Amphitheater; Dump Station; Camp Store; Staff/Volunteer Host Onsite; Potable Water (Yes - seasonal); Ice Available For Sale; Firewood For Sale; Food Storage Lockers
- Accessibility: 78 ADA camp sites including tent and RV sites; accessible sites and all restrooms wheelchair accessible; paved roads
- Reservation URL: https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/251833; Phone: 207-288-1300

### Duck Harbor Campground
- Location: Isle au Haut, PO Box 177, Bar Harbor, ME 04609 (rugged island off the coast of Stonington; accessible only by mailboat; no roads)
- Sites: 5 total (5 tent-only / walk-boat-to lean-to shelters); max stay 3 nights, one stay per calendar year, 6 people per campsite; no pets; all trash carried out; no generators; quiet hours 10 p.m. to 8 a.m.; check-in/check-out 11:00 a.m.
- Fees: Tent/Shelter Non-Electric $20.00/night (max 6 people)
- Amenities: The park service campground listing returned no explicit amenity list for Duck Harbor (amenities field came back empty); known from listing: composting toilets (seasonal), potable water (seasonal), no showers, food storage required (carry out trash)
- Accessibility: not wheelchair-accessible; rocky/uneven terrain, narrow trails, sheltered entries stepped
- Reservation URL: https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/264293; Phone: 207-335-5551

Reservations: all campsites must be reserved in advance via Recreation.gov (not by phone or in-person). Blackwoods/Seawall/Schoodic Woods: 90% of sites released 6 months ahead on the first of each month at 10 am EST; remaining 10% released 10 am 14 days ahead of a desired arrival date on a rolling basis. Duck Harbor: reservations released 10 am April 1 for the entire season, 7 months in advance.

## Visitor centers (6 returned)

2. **Sieur de Monts Nature Center** (47 Sweet Water Circle, Bar Harbor, ME 04609) — Visitor services, hiking information, interactive exhibits; trailhead for many hiking trails. 9:00AM-5:00PM daily (June 14 through Oct 12, 2026).

4. **Islesford Historical Museum** (Little Cranberry Island, Islesford, ME 04646) — Cultural history exhibits, life on Cranberry Isles from 1700s to present. 10:30AM-4:00PM daily; closes for season Sep 19, 2026. Accessible only by boat.
5. **Schoodic Institute Welcome Center at Rockefeller Hall** (9 Atterbury Circle, Winter Harbor, ME 04693) — Exhibits about the old Navy base and park information. 10:00AM-5:00PM daily (May 1 through Oct 26).
