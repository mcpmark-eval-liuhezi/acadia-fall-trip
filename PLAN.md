# Acadia Fall Trip — Plan

Park code: `acad`

## Park overview

- **Name:** Acadia National Park
- **Designation:** National Park
- **Park code:** acad
- **State:** ME (Maine)
- **Location:** 44.409286, -68.247501 (Mount Desert Island, Bar Harbor, ME 04609; 25 Visitor Center Road, Hulls Cove Visitor Center)
- **Description:** Acadia National Park protects the natural beauty of the highest rocky headlands along the Atlantic coastline of the United States, an abundance of habitats, and a rich cultural heritage. At 4 million visits a year, it's one of the top 10 most-visited national parks in the United States. Visitors enjoy 27 miles of historic motor roads, 158 miles of hiking trails, and 45 miles of carriage roads.
- **Weather (fall):** 30-70F (-1-21C). First frost typically mid-October; first snowfall begins in November and can continue through April (average accumulation 73 in / 185 cm).
- **Park URL:** https://www.nps.gov/acad/index.htm
- **Contacts:** 207-288-3338 / acadia_information@nps.gov

### Duck Harbor Campground
- Location: Isle au Haut, PO Box 177, Bar Harbor, ME 04609 (rugged island off the coast of Stonington; accessible only by mailboat; no roads)
- Sites: 5 total (5 tent-only / walk-boat-to lean-to shelters); max stay 3 nights, one stay per calendar year, 6 people per campsite; no pets; all trash carried out; no generators; quiet hours 10 p.m. to 8 a.m.; check-in/check-out 11:00 a.m.
- Fees: Tent/Shelter Non-Electric $20.00/night (max 6 people)
- Amenities: The park service campground listing returned no explicit amenity list for Duck Harbor (amenities field came back empty); known from listing: composting toilets (seasonal), potable water (seasonal), no showers, food storage required (carry out trash)
- Accessibility: not wheelchair-accessible; rocky/uneven terrain, narrow trails, sheltered entries stepped
- Reservation URL: https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/264293; Phone: 207-335-5551

Reservations: all campsites must be reserved in advance via Recreation.gov (not by phone or in-person). Blackwoods/Seawall/Schoodic Woods: 90% of sites released 6 months ahead on the first of each month at 10 am EST; remaining 10% released 10 am 14 days ahead of a desired arrival date on a rolling basis. Duck Harbor: reservations released 10 am April 1 for the entire season, 7 months in advance.

## Visitor centers (6 returned)

1. **Hulls Cove Visitor Center** (25 Visitor Center Road, Bar Harbor, ME 04609) — main visitor contact station and transportation hub May into October; adjacent to Park Loop Road entrance; key stop for fare-free Island Explorer bus. 52 steps to main entrance; separate accessible entrance. Hours: daily 8:30 AM - 4:30 PM. Winter info moves to Bar Harbor Chamber of Commerce (2 Cottage St.).
2. **Sieur de Monts Nature Center** (47 Sweet Water Circle, Bar Harbor, ME 04609) — visitor services, hiking information, interactive exhibits; trailhead for many hikes. Hours: daily 9 AM - 5 PM (June 14 through Oct 12, 2026).
3. **Acadia Gateway Center** (Route 3, Trenton, ME 04605) — intermodal transit and welcome center; Island Explorer bus stop, day-use parking, 11,000 sq ft welcome center with tourism info, store, waiting area, restrooms. Owned by State of Maine, operated with NPS partnership. Hours: 8 AM - 6 PM, Wed May 20 to Mon Oct 12, 2026.
4. **Islesford Historical Museum** (Little Cranberry Island, Islesford, ME 04646) — cultural history, life on/around Cranberry Isles from 1700s to present; accessible only by boat. Hours: daily 10:30 AM - 4:00 PM (closes for season Sep 19, 2026).
5. **Schoodic Institute Welcome Center at Rockefeller Hall** (9 Atterbury Circle, Winter Harbor, ME 04693) — exhibits about the old Navy base and park information; on Schoodic Peninsula off the one-way loop road. Hours: daily 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM (May 1 through Oct 26, 2025).
6. **Winter Information: Bar Harbor Chamber of Commerce** (2 Cottage Street, Bar Harbor, ME 04609) — winter/spring info and some pass sales; open 7 days a week 8 am to 4 pm, typically Nov 1 into early May; holiday closures on Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, New Year's Eve, and New Year's Day.
