## Park overview

- **Name:** Acadia National Park
- **Park code:** `acad`
- **State:** Maine (ME)
- **Designation:** National Park
- **Official description:** "Acadia National Park protects the natural beauty of the highest rocky headlands along the Atlantic coastline of the United States, an abundance of habitats, and a rich cultural heritage. At 4 million visits a year, it's one of the top 10 most-visited national parks in the United States. Visitors enjoy 27 miles of historic motor roads, 158 miles of hiking trails, and 45 miles of carriage roads."
- **Official site:** https://www.nps.gov/acad/index.htm
- **Coordinates:** 44.409286, -68.247501 (Mount Desert Island)
- **Operating hours:** Open year-round, all day, every day
- **Fall weather (official):** Fall temperatures range from 30–70°F (-1–21°C). Typically the first frost is in mid-October and first snowfall begins in November (can continue through April with an average accumulation of 73 inches / 185 cm). Pack cold-weather and rain layers.
- **Entrance fees (for budgeting):**
  - Private vehicle: $35.00 (valid 7 days)
  - Motorcycle: $30.00 (valid 7 days, up to 2 motorcycles / 4 passengers)
  - Per person (foot, bike, pedestrian): $20.00 (valid 7 days; youth 15 and under free)
  - Timed-entry vehicle reservation for Cadillac Summit Road: $6.00 (per vehicle; road closes to vehicles at 9 pm; no re-entry; no assigned parking)
  - Nonresident fee: $100.00 for each non-US resident aged 16+ (waived with Annual or America the Beautiful Pass)

1. **Drop-in Stewardship Volunteering with Friends of Acadia** — Drop-in volunteer program every Tuesday, Thursday, and Saturday (through the end of October). Crew-leader-led work: cutting back vegetation, clearing drainage features, building bogwalk, tread work, weeding carriage roads. Projects run 8:15 a.m.–12:15 p.m., weather permitting; no experience or reservation required. Location: Hulls Cove Storage Facility, near the Hulls Cove Visitor Center. (Note: this is the same location as 2024; Park HQ is under construction.)
2. **Wetland Revival – Walking the Path of Restoration** — Ranger-led, mostly level 2.3-mile walk through the Great Meadow, learning how Acadia, the Schoodic Institute, and Friends of Acadia are restoring a damaged wetland. Accessibility: sign language, live audio description, assistive listening devices available with 10 days' notice (207-288-3338, option 0, or acadia_information@nps.gov).
3. **EeeK of Ecology Halloween Festival for Children** — Trick-or-treat through learning stations about different National Parks at the Schoodic Institute campus (children 12 and under, with an adult).
4. **Take Pride in Acadia Day** — Volunteer day raking leaves out of carriage road drainage ditches; ends with chili, corn bread, cider, and cake.

## Campgrounds

The park service lists **4 campgrounds** for Acadia. Reservations for all are via Recreation.gov only (90% of sites released 6 months in advance on the first of each month at 10 am EST; the remaining 10% released at 10 am 14 days ahead of arrival date on a rolling basis). **All sites are reservable — no first-come, first-served camping.**

### 1. Blackwoods Campground

- **Location:** 155 Blackwoods Drive, Otter Creek, ME 04660 (44.30894, -68.20989) — east side of Mount Desert Island, ~5 miles (8 km) south of Bar Harbor on Route 3; all sites wooded, within a 10-minute walk of the ocean
- **Season:** Open seasonally, usually early May to mid-October
- **Sites:** 281 total, all reservable (221 tent-only, 60 RV, 4 group; no electrical hookups)
- **Fees:** $30/night tent-only or RV non-electric; $60/night group tent area
- **Amenities:** Trash/Recycling Collection, Toilets (Flush Toilets - seasonal), Internet Connectivity, Showers (None), Cell Phone Reception, Laundry, Amphitheater, Dump Station, Camp Store, Staff/Volunteer Host Onsite, Potable Water (Yes - seasonal), Ice Available For Sale, Firewood For Sale, Food Storage Lockers
- **Reservation URL:** https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/232508
- **Phone:** 207-288-3274
- **Fall note:** Season typically ends mid-October — confirm it's open for the trip dates.

### 2. Seawall Campground

- **Location:** 664 Seawall Rd, Southwest Harbor, ME 04679 (44.24156, -68.30514) — west side of Mount Desert Island, ~4 miles (6 km) south of Southwest Harbor; ~18 miles from Bar Harbor; all sites within a short walking distance of the coastline
- **Season:** Open seasonally, typically from the Wednesday before Memorial Day to Columbus Day
- **Sites:** 202 total, all reservable (43 tent-only, 59 RV, 98 walk-in, 5 group; no electrical hookups)
- **Fees:** $30/night standard/RV/tent non-electric; $22/night walk-in; $60/night group tent area
- **Amenities:** Trash/Recycling Collection, Toilets (Flush Toilets - seasonal), Internet Connectivity, Showers (None), Cell Phone Reception, Laundry, Amphitheater, Dump Station, Camp Store, Staff/Volunteer Host Onsite, Potable Water (Yes - seasonal), Ice Available For Sale, Firewood For Sale, Food Storage Lockers
- **Reservation URL:** https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/234674
- **Phone:** 207-288-3338
- **Ranger station:** generally open 9 am–8 pm during peak season (subject to staffing)
- **Fall note:** Season typically ends Columbus Day — confirm it's open for the trip dates.

### 3. Schoodic Woods Campground

- **Location:** 54 Farview Dr, Winter Harbor, ME 04693 (44.38006, -68.06659) — Schoodic Peninsula, 1.5 miles (2.5 km) southeast of Winter Harbor; approximately 60–70 minutes from Bar Harbor and other areas of Mount Desert Island
- **Season:** Open seasonally, from the Wednesday before Memorial Day Weekend until Columbus Day
- **Sites:** 89 total, all reservable (13 tent-only, 41 RV, 78 with electrical hookups, 2 group, 9 hike-in)
- **Fees:** $30/night standard electric or tent-only electric; $40/night RV electric + water; $36/night RV electric no water; $22/night hike-in; $60/night group tent site
- **Amenities:** Trash/Recycling Collection, Toilets (Flush Toilets - seasonal, Vault Toilets - seasonal), Internet Connectivity, Showers (None), Cell Phone Reception, Laundry, Amphitheater, Dump Station, Camp Store, Staff/Volunteer Host Onsite, Potable Water (Yes - seasonal), Ice Available For Sale, Firewood For Sale, Food Storage Lockers
- **Reservation URL:** https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/251833
- **Phone:** 207-288-1300
- **Fall note:** Season typically ends Columbus Day — confirm it's open for the trip dates.

### 4. Duck Harbor Campground

- **Location:** Isle au Haut (mailing: Acadia National Park, Attn: Duck Harbor Campground, PO Box 177, Bar Harbor, ME 04609; 44.02779, -68.65353) — rugged island off the coast of Stonington, Maine; remote and inaccessible to automobiles; linked to the mainland by mailboat
- **Sites:** 5 lean-to shelter sites, all reservable, all walk/boat-to
- **Fees:** $20.00/night (Tent/Shelter Non-Electric; maximum 6 people per site)
- **Amenities:** Trash/Recycling Collection, Toilets (Composting Toilets - seasonal), Internet Connectivity, Showers (None), Cell Phone Reception, Laundry, Amphitheater, Dump Station, Camp Store, Staff/Volunteer Host Onsite, Potable Water (Yes - seasonal), Ice Available For Sale, Firewood For Sale, Food Storage Lockers
- **Reservation URL:** https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/264293
- **Phone:** 207-335-5551
- **Key regulations:** No pets allowed. Maximum stay of three nights; one stay per calendar year. Six people per campsite. All trash must be carried out. Do not bring your own firewood. Check-in/check-out time is 11:00 am. Quiet hours 10 p.m.–8 a.m. Generator use prohibited. Late arrivals must call (207) 335-5551.
- **Fall note:** Only 5 boat-access-only sites, and all reservations for the entire season are released at once on April 1 at 10 am — verify availability before committing. Not wheelchair accessible (rocky, uneven terrain; stepped shelter entrances).

## Visitor centers

The park service lists **6 visitor contact locations**:

### 1. Hulls Cove Visitor Center (main)

- **Location:** 25 Visitor Center Road, Bar Harbor, ME 04609 (44.40854, -68.24614)
- **Hours:** 8:30 AM – 4:30 PM daily (all seven days). Summer season typically runs from the first week of May to the end of October; in winter, visitor information moves to the Bar Harbor Chamber of Commerce.
- **Access:** Close to Bar Harbor off Route 3, adjacent to an entrance for the 27-mile Park Loop Road and a key stop on the fare-free Island Explorer bus system. Visitors climb 52 steps to the main entrance; a separate accessible entrance is available.
- **Phone:** 207-288-3338

### 2. Sieur de Monts Nature Center


- **Location:** 47 Sweet Water Circle, Bar Harbor, ME 04609 (44.36211, -68.20785)
- **Hours:** 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM daily, June 14 through Oct 12, 2026
- **Access:** Reached from Park Loop Road or ME 3 (Main Street) south of Bar Harbor. Rangers present spring, summer, and fall months.

### 3. Acadia Gateway Center


- **Location:** Acadia Gateway Center, Gateway Center Drive, Trenton, ME 04605 (44.47055, -68.36618)
- **Hours:** 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM, Wednesday May 20 to Monday Oct 12, 2026
- **Access:** Intermodal transit and welcome center on Route 3 in Trenton, three miles north of Mount Desert Island. Island Explorer bus stop, day-use parking, tourism information, store, waiting area, and restrooms. Owned by the State of Maine, operated by Maine DOT in partnership with NPS, Downeast Transportation Inc., and Maine Office of Tourism.

### 4. Islesford Historical Museum


- **Location:** Little Cranberry Island, Islesford, ME 04646 (44.26159, -68.23982)
- **Hours:** 10:30 AM – 4:00 PM daily; closes for the season on Sep 19, 2026
- **Access:** Accessible only by boat, located to the left of the Town Pier in Islesford. Exhibits showcase life on and around the Cranberry Isles from the 1700s to present.

### 5. Schoodic Institute Welcome Center at Rockefeller Hall


- **Location:** 9 Atterbury Circle, Winter Harbor, ME 04693 (44.33608, -68.05914)
- **Hours:** 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM daily (seven days per week); per its listing: May 1 through Oct 26 (2025 season) Rockefeller Hall exhibits and Welcome Center are open seven days per week
- **Access:** Located on the Schoodic Peninsula off the one-way loop road (follow signs from ME 186 into Winter Harbor past the Schoodic Education and Research Center, then Schoodic Loop Road → Arey Cove Drive → first right onto Acadia Drive → second left onto Atterbury Circle).

### 6. Winter Information: Bar Harbor Chamber of Commerce

- **Location:** 2 Cottage Street, Bar Harbor, ME 04609 (44.38975, -68.20477)
- **Hours:** 8:00 AM – 4:00 PM daily, typically Nov 1 into early May (holiday closures: Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, New Year's Eve, New Year's Day)
- **Access:** Used as the park's visitor information and pass-sales location during winter and spring. Full-day holiday closures noted.
