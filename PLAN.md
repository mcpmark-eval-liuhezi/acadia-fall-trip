# Acadia Fall Trip — Plan

Planning home base for the hiking group's autumn trip to **Acadia National Park**.

> Research pulled from the National Park Service on 2026-10-21. Park code: `acad`.

## Park overview

- **Name:** Acadia National Park
- **Park code:** `acad`
- **State:** Maine (ME)
- **Designation:** National Park
- **Official description:** "Acadia National Park protects the natural beauty of the highest rocky headlands along the Atlantic coastline of the United States, an abundance of habitats, and a rich cultural heritage. At 4 million visits a year, it's one of the top 10 most-visited national parks in the United States. Visitors enjoy 27 miles of historic motor roads, 158 miles of hiking trails, and 45 miles of carriage roads."
- **Official site:** https://www.nps.gov/acad/index.htm
- **Coordinates:** 44.409286, -68.247501
- **Park grounds:** Open year-round, all day, every day
- **Seasonal weather note (fall):** Fall temperatures range from 30–70°F (-1–21°C). Typically the first frost is in mid-October, and first snowfall begins in November.

### Entrance fees (for budgeting)

- Private vehicle: $35.00 (valid 7 days)
- Motorcycle: $30.00 (valid 7 days, up to 2 motorcycles / 4 passengers)
- Per person (foot, bike, pedestrian): $20.00 (valid 7 days; youth 15 and under free)
- Timed-entry vehicle reservation for Cadillac Summit Road: $6.00
- Nonresident fee (non-US residents 16+, in addition to standard entrance fee): $100.00

**Details:**

1. **Drop-in Stewardship Volunteering with Friends of Acadia** — Drop-in volunteer program (Tuesdays, Thursdays, and Saturdays through the end of October). Crew-leader-led work: cutting back vegetation, clearing drainage features, building bogwalk, tread work, weeding carriage roads. Projects run 8:15 a.m.–12:15 p.m., weather permitting; no experience or reservation required.
2. **Wetland Revival – Walking the Path of Restoration** — Ranger-led, mostly level 2.3-mile walk through the Great Meadow, on how the park, the Schoodic Institute, and Friends of Acadia are restoring a damaged wetland.
3. **EeeK of Ecology Halloween Festival for Children** — Trick-or-treat through National Park learning stations at the Schoodic Institute campus (children 12 and under, with an adult).
4. **Take Pride in Acadia Day** — Volunteer day raking leaves out of carriage road drainage ditches; ends with chili, corn bread, cider, and cake.

## Campgrounds

The park service lists **4 campgrounds** for Acadia:

### Blackwoods Campground
- **Location:** 155 Blackwoods Drive, Otter Creek, ME 04660 (44.30894, -68.20989) — east side of Mount Desert Island, ~5 miles south of Bar Harbor on Route 3; all sites within a 10-minute walk of the ocean
- **Season:** Early May to mid-October (seasonal)
- **Sites:** 281 total, all reservable (221 tent-only, 60 RV, 4 group)
- **Fees:** $30/night tent or RV (non-electric); $60/night group tent area
- **Reservations:** Recreation.gov — 90% of sites released 6 months in advance on the first of each month at 10 am EST; remaining 10% released 14 days ahead
- **Amenities:** Trash/Recycling Collection, Toilets (Flush Toilets - seasonal), Internet Connectivity, Showers (None), Cell Phone Reception, Laundry, Amphitheater, Dump Station, Camp Store, Staff/Volunteer Host Onsite, Potable Water (Yes - seasonal), Ice Available For Sale, Firewood For Sale, Food Storage Lockers

### Seawall Campground
- **Location:** 664 Seawall Rd, Southwest Harbor, ME 04679 (44.24156, -68.30514) — west side of Mount Desert Island, ~4 miles south of Southwest Harbor, ~18 miles from Bar Harbor; all sites within a short walk of the coastline
- **Season:** Wednesday before Memorial Day to Columbus Day
- **Sites:** 202 total, all reservable (43 tent-only, 59 RV, 98 walk-in, 5 group)
- **Fees:** $30/night standard/RV/tent (non-electric); $22/night walk-in; $60/night group tent area
- **Reservations:** Recreation.gov — same release schedule as Blackwoods
- **Amenities:** Trash/Recycling Collection, Toilets (Flush Toilets - seasonal), Internet Connectivity, Showers (None), Cell Phone Reception, Laundry, Amphitheater, Dump Station, Camp Store, Staff/Volunteer Host Onsite, Potable Water (Yes - seasonal), Ice Available For Sale, Firewood For Sale, Food Storage Lockers

### Schoodic Woods Campground
- **Location:** 54 Farview Dr, Winter Harbor, ME 04693 (44.38006, -68.06659) — Schoodic Peninsula, 1.5 miles southeast of Winter Harbor; ~60–70 minutes from Bar Harbor
- **Season:** Wednesday before Memorial Day Weekend until Columbus Day
- **Sites:** 89 total, all reservable (13 tent-only, 41 RV, 78 with electrical hookups, 2 group, 9 hike-in)
- **Fees:** $30/night standard electric or tent-only electric; $40/night RV electric + water; $36/night RV electric no water; $22/night hike-in; $60/night group tent site
- **Reservations:** Recreation.gov — same release schedule as Blackwoods
- **Amenities:** Trash/Recycling Collection, Toilets (Flush Toilets - seasonal, Vault Toilets - seasonal), Internet Connectivity, Showers (None), Cell Phone Reception, Laundry, Amphitheater, Dump Station, Camp Store, Staff/Volunteer Host Onsite, Potable Water (Yes - seasonal), Ice Available For Sale, Firewood For Sale, Food Storage Lockers

### Duck Harbor Campground
- **Location:** Isle au Haut (mailing: Acadia National Park, Attn: Duck Harbor Campground, PO Box 177, Bar Harbor, ME 04609; 44.02779, -68.65353) — rugged island off the coast of Stonington, ME; accessible only by mailboat, no automobiles
- **Sites:** 5 lean-to shelter sites, all reservable, all walk/boat-to
- **Fees:** $20/night (Tent/Shelter Non-Electric, max 6 people)
- **Reservations:** Required; released 7 months in advance, all released at 10 am April 1 for the entire season
- **Key regulations:** No pets; max 3-night stay; one stay per calendar year; pack out all trash; 11:00am check-in/check-out; generators prohibited; quiet hours 10 p.m.–8 a.m.
- **Amenities:** Trash/Recycling Collection, Toilets (Composting Toilets - seasonal), Internet Connectivity, Showers (None), Cell Phone Reception, Laundry, Amphitheater, Dump Station, Camp Store, Staff/Volunteer Host Onsite, Potable Water (Yes - seasonal), Ice Available For Sale, Firewood For Sale, Food Storage Lockers
- **Accessibility note:** Not wheelchair accessible; terrain is rocky and uneven

### Seasonal-closure caveat for a fall trip
Blackwoods, Seawall, and Schoodic Woods all close for the season by mid-October (Columbus Day), so for late-fall dates the park service listings indicate these campgrounds will not be open. Duck Harbor is the only backcountry option, and it's boat-access-only with 5 sites.
