# Acadia Fall Trip — Planning Home Base

**Park code: `acad`** — Acadia National Park, Maine

## Park overview

- **Name:** Acadia National Park
- **Designation:** National Park
- **Park code:** acad
- **State:** ME (Maine)
- **Location:** 44.409286, -68.247501 (Mount Desert Island)
- **Official description (NPS):** "Acadia National Park protects the natural beauty of the highest rocky headlands along the Atlantic coastline of the United States, an abundance of habitats, and a rich cultural heritage. At 4 million visits a year, it's one of the top 10 most-visited national parks in the United States. Visitors enjoy 27 miles of historic motor roads, 158 miles of hiking trails, and 45 miles of carriage roads."

Fall weather (NPS): fall temperatures range from 30-70F (-1-21C); first frost typically mid-October; first snowfall begins in November.

## Campgrounds

4 campgrounds returned for Acadia by the NPS campground listing:

### Blackwoods Campground
- **Location:** 155 Blackwoods Drive, Otter Creek, ME 04660 (44.308944, -68.209885)
- **Description:** East side of Mount Desert Island, ~5 miles (8 km) south of Bar Harbor on Route 3. All sites wooded and within a 10-minute walk of the ocean. Majority of sites for small and large tents; others accommodate RVs.
- **Amenities:** Trash/Recycling Collection; Toilets (Flush Toilets - seasonal); Internet Connectivity; Showers (None); Cell Phone Reception; Laundry; Amphitheater; Dump Station; Camp Store; Staff/Volunteer Host Onsite; Potable Water (Yes - seasonal); Ice Available For Sale; Firewood For Sale; Food Storage Lockers
- **Sites:** 281 reservable (221 tent only, 60 RV only, 4 group); 0 electrical hookups
- **Season:** usually early May to mid-October
- **Fees:** $30.00 tent only non-electric; $30.00 RV non-electric; $60.00 group tent only area non-electric
- **Reservations:** 90% released 6 months in advance on the first of each month at 10 am EST; remaining 10% at 10 am 14 days ahead of arrival, rolling. All via Recreation.gov (campground 232508).

### Seawall Campground
- **Location:** 664 Seawall Rd, Southwest Harbor, ME 04679 (44.241565, -68.305144)
- **Description:** West side of Mount Desert Island, ~4 miles (6 km) south of Southwest Harbor; ~18 miles from Bar Harbor. All sites within a short walking distance of the coastline.
- **Amenities:** Trash/Recycling Collection; Toilets (Flush Toilets - seasonal); Internet Connectivity; Showers (None); Cell Phone Reception; Laundry; Amphitheater; Dump Station; Camp Store; Staff/Volunteer Host Onsite; Potable Water (Yes - seasonal); Ice Available For Sale; Firewood For Sale; Food Storage Lockers
- **Sites:** 202 reservable (43 tent only, 59 RV only, 98 walk/boat-to, 5 group); 0 electrical hookups
- **Season:** typically the Wednesday before Memorial Day to Columbus Day; ranger station generally 9 am-8 pm during peak season
- **Fees:** $30.00 RV non-electric; $30.00 standard non-electric; $30.00 tent only non-electric; $60.00 group tent only area non-electric; $22.00 walk-in
- **Reservations:** same 90/10 Recreation.gov rolling release schedule (campground 234674).

### Schoodic Woods Campground
- **Location:** 54 Farview Dr, Winter Harbor, ME 04693 (44.380063, -68.066594)
- **Description:** Newest campground in the park, 1.5 miles (2.5 km) southeast of Winter Harbor on the Schoodic Peninsula; ~60-70 minutes from Bar Harbor and other areas of Mount Desert Island.
- **Amenities:** Trash/Recycling Collection; Toilets (Flush Toilets - seasonal, Vault Toilets - seasonal); Internet Connectivity; Showers (None); Cell Phone Reception; Laundry; Amphitheater; Dump Station; Camp Store; Staff/Volunteer Host Onsite; Potable Water (Yes - seasonal); Ice Available For Sale; Firewood For Sale; Food Storage Lockers
- **Sites:** 89 reservable (13 tent only, 41 RV only, 78 electrical hookups, 9 walk/boat-to, 2 group)
- **Season:** Wednesday before Memorial Day Weekend until Columbus Day
- **Fees:** $22.00 hike-in; $30.00 standard electric; $30.00 tent only electric; $36.00 RV electric no water; $40.00 RV electric and water; $60.00 group tent site
- **Reservations:** same 90/10 Recreation.gov rolling release schedule (campground 251833).

### Duck Harbor Campground
- **Location:** Acadia National Park, Attn: Duck Harbor Campground, PO Box 177, Bar Harbor, ME 04609 (44.027795, -68.653529) — on Isle au Haut, a rugged island off the coast of Stonington; remote and inaccessible to automobiles; linked to the mainland by mailboat.
- **Description:** 5 sites with lean-to shelters; great launch point for paddling excursions.
- **Amenities:** The NPS campground listing for Duck Harbor returned site, fee, and regulation data but the amenities array was truncated in the API response. Confirmed from the listing: 5 lean-to shelter sites (all tent/shelter, walk/boat-to), no electrical hookups.
- **Sites:** 5 reservable (all tent/shelter walk/boat-to); 0 first-come-first-served.
- **Season/fees:** Tent/Shelter Non-Electric $20.00/night; maximum 6 people per campsite.
- **Regulations:** Reservations required; available 7 months in advance; all reservations released at 10 am April 1 for the entire season. No pets. Maximum stay 3 nights. One stay per calendar year. All trash carried out. No outside firewood. Check-in/check-out 11:00am. Quiet hours 10 pm-8 am. Generator use prohibited.
- **Reservations:** via Recreation.gov (campground 264293).

## Visitor centers

6 visitor centers/locations returned for `acad`:

1. **Hulls Cove Visitor Center** — 25 Visitor Center Road, Bar Harbor, ME 04609. 8:30AM-4:30PM daily. Main visitor contact station and transportation hub May into October; adjacent to Park Loop Road entrance; key stop for fare-free Island Explorer bus; 52 steps to main entrance (separate accessible entrance available). Winter info moves to the Bar Harbor Chamber of Commerce.
2. **Sieur de Monts Nature Center** — 47 Sweet Water Circle, Bar Harbor, ME 04609. 9:00AM-5:00PM daily, June 14 through Oct 12, 2026. Visitor services, hiking information, interactive exhibits; trailhead for many hiking trails; rangers present spring, summer, and fall.
3. **Acadia Gateway Center** — Route 3, Trenton, ME 04605. 8:00AM-6:00PM, Wed May 20 to Mon Oct 12, 2026. Intermodal transit and welcome center; Island Explorer bus stop, day-use parking, 11,000 sq ft welcome center with tourism information, store, waiting area, and restrooms. Note: the NPS listing shows Monday and Tuesday as "8:00PM-6:00PM," which appears to be a data typo; treated as 8:00AM-6:00PM.
4. **Islesford Historical Museum** — Little Cranberry Island, Islesford, ME 04646. 10:30AM-4:00PM daily; closes for the season Sep 19, 2026. Cultural history of Islesford and the Cranberry Isles from the 1700s to present; accessible only by boat.
5. **Schoodic Institute Welcome Center at Rockefeller Hall** — 9 Atterbury Circle, Winter Harbor, ME 04693. 10:00AM-5:00PM daily; per the NPS listing, May 1 through Oct 26, 2025 (the year in the source appears to be a template artifact). Exhibits about the old Navy base and park information; on the Schoodic Peninsula off the one-way loop road.
6. **Winter Information: Bar Harbor Chamber of Commerce** — 2 Cottage Street, Bar Harbor, ME 04609. 8:00AM-4:00PM daily, typically Nov 1 into early May; full-day holiday closures on Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, New Year's Eve, and New Year's Day.
