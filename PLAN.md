# Acadia Fall Trip — Plan

Home base for the hiking group's autumn trip to Acadia National Park (park code: **acad**, Maine).

## Park overview

- **Name:** Acadia National Park
- **Designation:** National Park
- **Park code:** acad
- **State:** Maine (ME)
- **Location:** 44.409286, -68.247501 (Mount Desert Island)
- **Official description:** Acadia National Park protects the natural beauty of the highest rocky headlands along the Atlantic coastline of the United States, an abundance of habitats, and a rich cultural heritage. At 4 million visits a year, it's one of the top 10 most-visited national parks in the United States. Visitors enjoy 27 miles of historic motor roads, 158 miles of hiking trails, and 45 miles of carriage roads.
- **Fall weather:** 30-70F (-1-21C); first frost typically mid-October; first snowfall begins in November and can continue through April (average accumulation 73 inches / 185 cm).
- **Entrance fees:** $35 private vehicle (7 days); $30 motorcycle (up to 2, 4 total passengers); $20 per person (bike/hike/pedestrian; youth 15 and under free). $6 timed-entry vehicle reservation for Cadillac Summit Road. Non-US residents 16+ pay an additional $100 nonresident fee unless holding an Annual or America the Beautiful Pass.
- **Contacts:** Voice 207-288-3338; TTY 207-288-8800; email acadia_information@nps.gov.

## Campgrounds (4 listed by the park service)

1. **Blackwoods Campground** — East side of Mount Desert Island, about 5 miles (8 km) south of Bar Harbor on Route 3; 155 Blackwoods Drive, Otter Creek, ME 04660 (44.308944, -68.209885). All sites wooded and within a 10-minute walk of the ocean; majority for small and large tents, other sites accommodate RVs. 281 sites (221 tent-only, 60 RV non-electric, 4 group); 0 electrical hookups. Open seasonally, usually early May to mid-October. Fees: Tent Only Non-electric $30, RV Non-electric $30, Group Tent Only Area $60. Amenities: Trash/Recycling Collection; Toilets (Flush Toilets - seasonal); Internet Connectivity; Showers (None); Cell Phone Reception; Laundry; Amphitheater; Dump Station; Camp Store; Staff/Volunteer Host Onsite; Potable Water (Yes - seasonal); Ice Available For Sale; Firewood For Sale; Food Storage Lockers. Accessibility: 12 accessible drive-in sites (RV max 35 ft); hard-packed walkways to wheelchair-accessible restrooms and amphitheater. Reservations via Recreation.gov (https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/232508). Phone: 207-288-3274.
2. **Seawall Campground** — West side of Mount Desert Island, about 4 miles (6 km) south of Southwest Harbor; approximately 18 miles from Bar Harbor; 664 Seawall Rd, Southwest Harbor, ME 04679 (44.241565, -68.305144). All sites within a short walking distance of the coastline. 202 sites (43 tent-only, 59 RV only, 98 walk/boat-to, 5 group); 0 electrical hookups. Open seasonally, typically from the Wednesday before Memorial Day to Columbus Day; ranger station generally open 9 am to 8 pm during peak season. Fees: RV Non-electric $30, Standard Non-electric $30, Tent Only Non-electric $30, Group Tent Only Area $60, Walk-in $22. Amenities: Trash/Recycling Collection; Toilets (Flush Toilets - seasonal); Internet Connectivity; Showers (None); Cell Phone Reception; Laundry; Amphitheater; Dump Station; Camp Store; Staff/Volunteer Host Onsite; Potable Water (Yes - seasonal); Ice Available For Sale; Firewood For Sale; Food Storage Lockers. Accessibility: 3 RV (35-ft max), 5 drive-in, 5 walk-in, one group site; paved road to amphitheater. Reservations via Recreation.gov (https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/234674). Phone: 207-288-3338.
3. **Schoodic Woods Campground** — Newest campground, 1.5 miles (2.5 km) southeast of Winter Harbor on the Schoodic Peninsula; approximately 60-70 minutes from Bar Harbor; 54 Farview Dr, Winter Harbor, ME 04693 (44.380063, -68.066594). 89 sites (13 tent-only, 41 RV only, 9 hike-in, 2 group); 78 electrical hookups. Open seasonally from the Wednesday before Memorial Day Weekend until Columbus Day. Fees: Hike-In Site $22, Standard Electric $30, Tent Only Electric $30, RV Electric No Water $36, RV Electric and Water $40, Group Tent Site $60. Amenities: Trash/Recycling Collection; Toilets (Flush Toilets - seasonal, Vault Toilets - seasonal); Internet Connectivity; Showers (None); Cell Phone Reception; Laundry; Amphitheater; Dump Station; Camp Store; Staff/Volunteer Host Onsite; Potable Water (Yes - seasonal); Ice Available For Sale; Firewood For Sale; Food Storage Lockers. Accessibility: 78 ADA camp sites including tent and RV sites; paved roads, all vehicles OK. Reservations via Recreation.gov (https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/251833). Phone: 2072881300.
4. **Duck Harbor Campground** — Located on Isle au Haut, a rugged island off the coast of Stonington, Maine; remote and inaccessible to automobiles; linked to the mainland by mailboat. Address on file: Acadia National Park, Attn: Duck Harbor Campground, PO Box 177, Bar Harbor, ME 04609 (44.027795, -68.653529). 5 sites with lean-to shelters; a great launch point for paddling excursions. Operating hours array was empty in the listing. Fee: Tent/Shelter Non-Electric $20/night (maximum 6 people per site). Reservations required, available 7 months in advance; all released at 10 am on April 1 for the entire season. Reservations via Recreation.gov (https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/264293). Phone: (207) 335-5551. Regulations: no pets; maximum stay 3 nights; one stay per calendar year; pack out all trash; no generators; quiet hours 10 pm to 8 am; check-in/check-out 11:00 am.

## Visitor centers

1. **Hulls Cove Visitor Center** — 25 Visitor Center Road, Bar Harbor, ME 04609 (44.408542, -68.246137). Acadia's main visitor contact station and transportation hub from May into October; adjacent to an entrance for the 27-mile Park Loop Road and a key stop for fare-free Island Explorer bus routes. 52 steps to the main entrance; separate accessible entrance. Open 8:30 AM - 4:30 PM daily; summer season typically first week of May to end of October. Phone 207-288-3338; TTY 207-288-8800.
2. **Sieur de Monts Nature Center** — 47 Sweet Water Circle, Bar Harbor, ME 04609 (44.362109, -68.207848). Visitor services, hiking information, and interactive exhibits; acts as a trailhead for many hiking trails. Rangers present spring, summer, and fall. Open 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily, June 14 through Oct 12, 2026. Phone 207-288-3338; Fax 207-288-8813; TTY 207-288-8800.
3. **Acadia Gateway Center** — Acadia Gateway Center, Gateway Center Drive, Trenton, ME 04605 (44.470549, -68.366182). Intermodal transit and welcome center on Route 3 in Trenton, three miles north of Mount Desert Island; Island Explorer bus stop, day-use parking, and an 11,000 sq ft welcome center with tourism information, store, waiting area, and restrooms. Owned by the State of Maine, operated by the Maine Department of Transportation in partnership with the National Park Service, Downeast Transportation Inc., and the Maine Office of Tourism. Hours 8:00 AM - 6:00 PM, Wed May 20 to Mon Oct 12, 2026 (the listing shows Monday as 8:00PM-6:00PM, likely a typo in the source data).
4. **Islesford Historical Museum** — Little Cranberry Island, Islesford, ME 04646 (44.261592, -68.239818). Accessible only by boat, located to the left of the Town Pier in Islesford; showcases life on and around the Cranberry Isles from the 1700s to present. Open 10:30 AM - 4:00 PM; closes for the season Sep 19, 2026.
5. **Schoodic Institute Welcome Center at Rockefeller Hall** — 9 Atterbury Circle, Winter Harbor, ME 04693 (44.336076, -68.059139). Located on the Schoodic Peninsula off the one-way loop road; exhibits about the old Navy base and park information. Open 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM daily; per the listing, May 1 through Oct 26, 2025.
6. **Winter Information: Bar Harbor Chamber of Commerce** — 2 Cottage Street, Bar Harbor, ME 04609 (44.389749, -68.204773). During winter and spring, information and sales of some passes; full-day holiday closures on Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, New Year's Eve, and New Year's Day. Open 8:00 AM - 4:00 PM daily, typically from Nov 1 into early May.
