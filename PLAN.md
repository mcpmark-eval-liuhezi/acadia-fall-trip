# Acadia Fall Trip — Planning Home Base

Park code: **acad** (Acadia National Park, Maine)

## Park Overview

- **Name:** Acadia National Park
- **Designation:** National Park
- **Park code:** acad
- **Location:** Mount Desert Island, Maine (lat 44.409286, lon -68.247501)
- **Official description:** Acadia National Park protects the natural beauty of the highest rocky headlands along the Atlantic coastline of the United States, an abundance of habitats, and a rich cultural heritage. At 4 million visits a year, it's one of the top 10 most-visited national parks in the United States. Visitors enjoy 27 miles of historic motor roads, 158 miles of hiking trails, and 45 miles of carriage roads.
- **Park URL:** https://www.nps.gov/acad/index.htm
- **Phone:** 207-288-3338 | **Email:** acadia_information@nps.gov

## Campgrounds

1. **Blackwoods Campground**
   - Location: 155 Blackwoods Drive, Otter Creek, ME 04660 (east side of Mount Desert Island, about 5 miles/8 km south of Bar Harbor on Route 3; all sites wooded and within a 10-minute walk of the ocean).
   - Amenities: Trash/Recycling Collection; Toilets (Flush Toilets – seasonal); Internet Connectivity; Showers (None); Cell Phone Reception; Laundry; Amphitheater; Dump Station; Camp Store; Staff/Volunteer Host Onsite; Potable Water (Yes – seasonal); Ice Available For Sale; Firewood For Sale; Food Storage Lockers.
   - 281 sites (221 tent-only, 60 RV non-electric, 4 group); open seasonally, usually early May to mid-October. Fees: $30 tent only non-electric, $30 RV non-electric, $60 group tent only area.
2. **Seawall Campground**
   - Location: 664 Seawall Rd, Southwest Harbor, ME 04679 (west side of Mount Desert Island, about 4 miles/6 km south of Southwest Harbor; approximately 18 miles from Bar Harbor; all sites within a short walking distance of the coastline).
   - Amenities: Trash/Recycling Collection; Toilets (Flush Toilets – seasonal); Internet Connectivity; Showers (None); Cell Phone Reception; Laundry; Amphitheater; Dump Station; Camp Store; Staff/Volunteer Host Onsite; Potable Water (Yes – seasonal); Ice Available For Sale; Firewood For Sale; Food Storage Lockers.
   - 202 sites (43 tent-only, 59 RV-only, 98 walk/boat-to, 5 group); open seasonally, typically from the Wednesday before Memorial Day to Columbus Day; ranger station generally 9 a.m. to 8 p.m. during peak season. Fees: $30 RV non-electric, $30 standard non-electric, $30 tent only non-electric, $22 walk-in, $60 group tent only area.
3. **Schoodic Woods Campground**
   - Location: 54 Farview Dr, Winter Harbor, ME 04693 (1.5 miles/2.5 km southeast of Winter Harbor on the Schoodic Peninsula; approximately 60–70 minutes from Bar Harbor and other areas of Mount Desert Island).
   - Amenities: Trash/Recycling Collection; Toilets (Flush Toilets – seasonal, Vault Toilets – seasonal); Internet Connectivity; Showers (None); Cell Phone Reception; Laundry; Amphitheater; Dump Station; Camp Store; Staff/Volunteer Host Onsite; Potable Water (Yes – seasonal); Ice Available For Sale; Firewood For Sale; Food Storage Lockers.
   - 89 sites (13 tent-only, 41 RV-only, 9 walk/boat-to, 2 group; 78 with electrical hookups); open seasonally from the Wednesday before Memorial Day Weekend until Columbus Day. Fees: $22 hike-in, $30 standard electric, $30 tent only electric, $36 RV electric no water, $40 RV electric and water, $60 group tent site.
4. **Duck Harbor Campground**
   - Location: Acadia National Park, Attn: Duck Harbor Campground, PO Box 177, Bar Harbor, ME 04609 — located on Isle au Haut, a rugged island off the coast of Stonington, Maine; remote and inaccessible to automobiles, linked to the mainland by mailboat.
   - Amenities: not returned in the park service listing (the amenity list came back empty for this campground); 5 sites with lean-to shelters; Tent/Shelter Non-Electric $20/night; reservations required, released 7 months in advance with all sites released at 10 a.m. on April 1 for the entire season; max 6 people per site, 3-night maximum stay, one stay per calendar year, no pets, no generators, pack out all trash.

## Visitor Centers

1. **Hulls Cove Visitor Center** — 25 Visitor Center Road, Bar Harbor, ME 04609. Hours: 8:30 AM–4:30 PM daily. Summer season roughly the first week of May to the end of October; during winter (typically end of October into early May) visitor information moves to the Bar Harbor Chamber of Commerce. 52 steps to the main entrance; separate accessible entrance. Adjacent to a Park Loop Road entrance; key stop for several Island Explorer bus routes.
2. **Sieur de Monts Nature Center** — 47 Sweet Water Circle, Bar Harbor, ME 04609. Hours: 9:00 AM–5:00 PM daily, June 14 through Oct 12, 2026. Visitor services, hiking information, and interactive exhibits; trailhead for many hiking trails in the Sieur de Monts area; rangers present spring, summer, and fall.
3. **Acadia Gateway Center** — Route 3, Trenton, ME 04605 (three miles north of Mount Desert Island). Hours: 8:00 AM–6:00 PM, Wed May 20 to Mon Oct 12, 2026. (Note: the listing shows Monday as 8:00 PM–6:00 PM, which appears to be a data typo.) Island Explorer bus stop, day-use parking, 11,000 sq ft welcome center with tourism information, store, waiting area, and restrooms; owned by the State of Maine, operated by Maine DOT in partnership with the National Park Service, Downeast Transportation Inc., and the Maine Office of Tourism.
4. **Islesford Historical Museum** — Little Cranberry Island, Islesford, ME 04646 (accessible only by boat). Hours: 10:30 AM–4:00 PM daily; the museum will close for the season on Sep 19, 2026. Exhibits showcase life on and around the Cranberry Isles from the 1700s to present.
5. **Schoodic Institute Welcome Center at Rockefeller Hall** — 9 Atterbury Circle, Winter Harbor, ME 04693. Hours: 10:00 AM–5:00 PM daily (listing dated May 1 through Oct 26, 2025). Exhibits about the old Navy base and park information; located on the Schoodic Peninsula off the one-way loop road.
6. **Winter Information: Bar Harbor Chamber of Commerce** — 2 Cottage Street, Bar Harbor, ME 04609. Hours: 8:00 AM–4:00 PM daily, typically from Nov 1 into early May; full-day holiday closures on Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, New Year's Eve, and New Year's Day.
